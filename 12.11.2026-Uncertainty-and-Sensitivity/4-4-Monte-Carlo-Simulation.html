Monte Carlo simulation

A Monte Carlo simulation propagates the uncertainty of the inputs through the model. Instead of calculating the result once with best-guess values, it calculates it thousands of times, each time with input values drawn at random from their probability distributions. The spread of the results is the uncertainty of the result. Illustrative data – do not use for studies.

  1. Give every uncertain input a distribution
  2. Draw one random value per input
  3. Calculate the model result
  4. Repeat many times
  5. Analyse the distribution of results
