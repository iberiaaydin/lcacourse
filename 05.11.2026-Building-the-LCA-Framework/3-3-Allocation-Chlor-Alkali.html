Allocation: one chlor-alkali cell, two products

The membrane cell makes chlorine (Product A) and sodium hydroxide (Product B) together. Use the sliders, or click a flow in the diagram, to decide which share of each flow goes to chlorine; the rest goes to sodium hydroxide. The line thickness inside the process shows the share. Whatever you choose, the allocated amounts always add up to the un-allocated inventory. Illustrative data – do not use for studies.
