Chlor-alkali membrane cell: brine and dilute sodium hydroxide are electrolysed into chlorine, hydrogen and concentrated sodium hydroxide, with a cation-exchange membrane that only lets sodium ions through.

Figure – Membrane cell for chlor-alkali electrolysis (schematic). Brine feeds the anode side and dilute NaOH the cathode side; the cation-exchange membrane lets only Na+ cross. One process gives three co-products (Cl2, H2 and NaOH), which makes it a classic multi-output process for allocation in LCA.
