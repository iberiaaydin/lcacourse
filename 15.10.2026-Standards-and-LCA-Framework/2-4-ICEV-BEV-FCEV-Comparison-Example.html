Assumptions
  • ICEV: petrol at 6.5 L/100 km. Tailpipe 150 g/km, fuel production (well-to-tank) 40 g/km.
  • BEV: 0.19 kWh/km from the wall, including charging and grid losses.
  • FCEV: 0.95 kg H₂/100 km. Hydrogen from electrolysis at 53 kWh/kg; storage is compression at 4 kWh/kg plus 0.6 kg CO₂e/kg for delivery.
  • Electricity intensity (g CO₂e/kWh): renewable mix 25, world grid mix 445, coal and gas 750, coal only 1000.
  • Manufacturing: ICEV 6.0 t, BEV 9.5 t, FCEV 8.5 t CO₂e per vehicle.
  • Values are typical literature figures for illustration, not measurements of specific models.
